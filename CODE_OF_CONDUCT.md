# Code of Conduct

We want contributors of every background and experience level to feel welcome.
This policy applies to project issues, reviews, discussions, events, and project
representation in public spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and explain feedback with concrete examples.
- Respect differences in identity, experience, communication style, and accessibility needs.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility when your behavior causes harm.
- Respect privacy and ask before sharing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and repeated disruption are unacceptable.
Retaliating against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately by email to buggybuckai@gmail.com, with "Kvlm conduct" in the
subject. Do not put private reports or identifying details in public issues. The project
currently has one maintainer, so a report about that maintainer cannot be handled
independently; say so in the report and it will be referred to another maintainer once
one exists.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports, protect the reporter's privacy as far as
practical, consider available evidence, and avoid handling reports in which they
have a conflict of interest. Possible responses include requesting a correction,
issuing a warning, restricting participation temporarily, or removing a participant.
The response should reflect severity and repeated behavior. A reporter or affected
participant may request reconsideration by another non-conflicted maintainer, when available.
